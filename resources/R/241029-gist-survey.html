4 Implementing best practices for a sustainable work environment Again, I have changed institutions over the last few years so it is difficult for me to assess progress over this time period. I do have the impression that continuous review and critique of promotion processes does impove guidance and what is considered, though I have not been on the decision-making side of this. I think that a number of institutions or centres are thinking about how to better onboard new team members so I am optimistic this will improve though I am not sure whetehr it actually has. I think support roles might be inteegrated where research grants/teams are large, which has generally been the case previously.
5 Implementing best practices for a sustainable work environment I have not seen a huge amount of change but we are starting some internal review processes which I hope will improve clarity around criteria for career progress in the near future
8 Implementing best practices for a sustainable work environment The persistent under-resourcing of projects leading to staff who are burnt out and over-worked has only persisted and there continues to be an expectation that staff work unpaid when there isn’t the long-term funding to support them for an appropriate duration or FTE. Institutions and research groups are unwilling to challenge funders on this, leading to cost cutting, impacts on staff mental health, high-turnovers and, ultimately, poorer quality work. In some ways, the fact that this has continued outside of the covid response context makes it even more insidious as it appears to have become the status quo. This does seem to be particularly pronounced in modelling / research intensive organisations and is not something I have noticed since moving to another field. Support staff roles fail to be properly understood within institutions like LSHTM (where I have experience) - or supported to develop. Support staff are burnt out, unsatisfied and under-appreciated. Research teams have little power to truly support these individuals to progress given the structural nature of the problems (unmalleable HR policies, etc.)
10 Implementing best practices for a sustainable work environment Clear probation/promotion criteria provided to new starters.
